Apple Photos says “Cannot Import” or asks about every duplicate: what is going on

You drag a large folder into Photos and get “Cannot Import 4,418 Items” with “Unknown error” against each file, or a never-ending “import this duplicate?” question. These are the usual causes, and a better way to import.

Download for Mac

Last reviewed 26 October 2026. Written by the Photo Clinic team from what we learned moving large photo libraries. See how we write these guides.

The two messages

“Would you like to import the following duplicate item?” means Photos found a picture in your library that matches the one you are importing. It shows both, and lets you tick Apply to all duplicates to answer once. If most of a folder is already in your library, you will see this again and again.

“Cannot Import… Unknown error.” means Photos tried and could not add the file. The dialog does not say why. The usual causes are below, with the most common first.

Why imports fail with “Unknown error”

  1. Low disk space. Photos needs room on the drive that holds the library, and Photos and macOS also use space on the drive your Mac starts from while importing. When either gets low, files near the end of a big import fail. Check both before a large import.
  2. The drive stopped or slept. Importing from an external drive that spins down, loses power, or is read at the same time as it is written to, can fail files at random.
  3. Damaged or cut-short files. A picture that never finished downloading cannot be added. These are usually a handful, not thousands.
  4. A name that does not match the content. A WebP or PNG saved as .jpg, or a format Photos does not support, is refused.
  5. Too many at once. A single huge drag overloads the import. Batches are kinder to Photos and to iCloud.

A better way: find out what is not in Photos first

Before importing, work out which pictures Photos does not have. Photos' own records list each picture's original name and exact size, so a folder can be compared with the library without importing anything, even for pictures that live only in iCloud. The ones that match are already there. A name that matches with a different size is a different version, such as an edited, resized or better copy. A RAW file is usually stored together with the JPEG of the same name.

Photo Clinic does this in What is not in Photos yet?: it shows your file next to the one in Photos, copies only the missing pictures into a new folder, and can move the ones Photos already has aside (never deleting them).

Importing a big library safely

  • Look at free space on the library drive and on your Mac first.
  • Try 20 photos and look at them in Photos before sending the rest.
  • Send in batches, oldest first, and let iCloud catch up between them.
  • Remember that Photos has no undo for an import. Keep your originals until you are happy.
