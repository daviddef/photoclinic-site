What is new in each version

Every release, newest first. The app tells you when an update is ready, so you never have to check.

Download for Mac

1.0.6 Latest

9 October 2026

Get photos out of an old library, find damaged files, and copy only the good ones.

  • Old library extractor (Tools, Utility). Turns an old Apple Photos, iPhoto or Aperture backup into a clean folder of copies, by album, year or year-month, with edited versions beside the originals and keywords, titles, ratings and places written into the copies. It skips exact and probable duplicates, shows a preview first, and has Undo.
  • Find damaged files lists photos and videos that are cut short, empty or will not open, and files that make a drive stop answering. It does not freeze when a drive stalls. Each row has copy-name, show and open-folder icons, and you can tag the damaged files in Finder.
  • Copy the good files copies the healthy photos and videos, with their .json files, to your Target and leaves the bad ones behind, with an optional move once each copy is verified. It is safe to run again after an interruption, and a stall lets you skip, try the next files or retry.
  • Log messages explain more. Ids in a log line get names where your Mac knows them (shared albums, photos) or a plain explanation where it does not, shown beside the untouched raw line with copy icons. Every finding has a line breakdown and a Plain or Technical wording switch.
  • Catalogue: 283 entries. 23 entries were researched again and 10 new ones added. Most are still marked low confidence and unverified, and each entry says what is inference.
  • Calmer and tidier: lines you have already sent are shown as sent, the Target shows on the Tools tab, sources are a tickable list, and the app repaints much less while it works. Live watch no longer hangs when a drive has stopped answering.

Download 1.0.6

1.0.5

8 October 2026

Fixes the menu hidden behind the side pane, and faster optional sharing.

  • The menu works again. In 1.0.4 the three-line menu, the style box and some dialogs opened hidden behind the side pane. They now open on top of it.
  • Optional sharing is quicker and easier to see. Lines you have agreed to share go out within a few minutes, there is a Send what is waiting now button, and the report inbox says what each status means.
  • More messages explained, including iCloud push tokens, server lock and zone messages, the file-transfer engine, and connection set-up failures.

Download 1.0.5

1.0.4

8 October 2026

A side pane that keeps watch, quick jobs, and many more messages explained.

  • A side pane with Activity, Live watch, Needs attention and the sync meter, each with a number on its tab. It collapses to a thin strip and you can drag it wider.
  • Live watch groups repeats into one card with a running total, flashes when something happens again, marks problems that fixed themselves with a green tick, and has a filter (the last five minutes by default). Every finding can show the log lines around it.
  • Quick jobs on the Fix screen and in Tools: fix metadata from Google's files only, dates only, places only, or fix in place. They tick only what that job needs.
  • Compare libraries now reads old iPhoto and Aperture libraries, opens with a plain verdict, and can copy what only one library has into year and album folders without changing either.
  • Check my photos also measures disk space and the sync meter, finds broken thumbnails, and fills a status row on Home.
  • Many more messages explained, each saying how we know. New: Save for review, and an optional, off-by-default way to share log lines Photo Clinic could not explain (cleaned first, Apple lines only, never linked to you).
  • Clearer and calmer: stray and missing files ignore what is already in Recently Deleted, the disk space tool shows the whole library's real size, no folder is chosen for you unless you ask, and the target drive's free space is shown.

Download 1.0.4

1.0.3

7 October 2026

One check for everything, one screen to fix, and findings sorted by how much they matter.

  • Check my photos: one button runs a read-only check of your photos, the Photos library, iCloud uploads, the logs and folder health, with a checklist, saved results and a status strip on Home.
  • Fix my photos: one screen for dates, places, captions, albums and Live Photos. Copy, move or fix in place.
  • Findings sorted by importance: Must resolve, Should review and Pure FYI. A passing network blip no longer looks like a problem, and many more log messages are explained in plain words.
  • Compare libraries (under Tools): compares two folders, Photos libraries, or old iPhoto and Aperture libraries, and can copy what only one has into year and album folders. Neither library is changed.
  • Library health score, a chart of library size and iCloud originals, a sortable process table, a Help me button and a Tools launcher.
  • The app can update itself from the next release.

Download 1.0.3

1.0.2

6 October 2026

Quieter, clearer log reading.

  • Harmless messages are left out. Photos, iCloud and macOS print many error-looking lines on a healthy Mac. The common harmless ones are now recognised and hidden, and the results say how many were left out.
  • Four more real problems explained: iCloud slowing the upload of photo-analysis data, a missing preview picture, an unreadable account list, and a dropped network connection.
  • The Photos Health monitor ignores the same harmless noise.

Download 1.0.2

1.0.1

6 October 2026

The first release: signed and notarised for Apple silicon Macs.

  • Fix a Google Takeout: dates, places, captions, people and favourites restored from Google's files, read straight from zips, with time zones, Live Photos, edited copies, albums and Motion Photos handled.
  • Dates and places from every clue: file names, folders, neighbouring photos and GPX tracks, plus offline place names for about 144,000 towns and cities.
  • Monitor your library: a Photos Health tab takes a reading every 15 minutes while the app is open, with trends and a plain verdict. If macOS blocks it, it says which permission to give and opens the settings.
  • Find what is not right: upload progress, log reading, live watch, a sync meter, a disk-space check, about 170 known problems and 17 step-by-step fix guides.
  • Combine and tidy: merge folders, remove exact copies, review look-alikes, convert old videos to MP4 with verification, and a photo health score.
  • Move to Apple Photos in batches, oldest first, waiting for iCloud between batches.
  • Five styles from Safest to I like risk, preview first, copy by default, stop and undo, and reports for every run.
