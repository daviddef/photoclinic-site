Versions

What is new in each version

Every release, newest first. The app tells you when an update is ready, so you never have to check.

Download for Mac

1.0.50 Latest

18 October 2026

Two small fixes to the confirmation windows and the plan.

  • Readable confirmations. The note under Do this for real? and similar notes in other windows were squashed into a narrow column and ran over the buttons. They now sit in a normal full-width box.
  • Plan wording. A plan with a single exact copy now says “1 exact copy skipped”, not “1 exact copies”.

Download 1.0.50

1.0.49

18 October 2026

Scan everything: one scan that ends in a plan you can do in one go or one job at a time.

  • Scan everything. Press it on Home or on Check. Photo Clinic reads what you chose, picks the recommended settings for it, and previews Fix, Tidy and Videos without changing anything.
  • Your plan. The result is one card with a row per job: its count (for example 36 files would be copied and fixed: 26 dates, 15 places), a tick box, Open to see the exact files, and Do this one.
  • Do the ticked ones. One summary, then the jobs run one after the other. Fix goes first and copies into your save folder, then the next jobs work on that finished library, not your originals.
  • Safe by default. Tidy deletes files, so it is never ticked for you and still asks you to type DELETE. Fix in place and moves still ask their own questions.
  • Fewer buttons that mean the same thing. Look at it now only appears for old libraries, struggling drives and mixed folders, where it still adds a plan; for a Takeout or a folder, Scan everything covers it.

Download 1.0.49

1.0.48

18 October 2026

A tidier app: Home starts with what you have, a slimmer header, and every picture on the site retaken.

  • Home starts with what you have. The top card lists what you chose and what Photo Clinic thinks each thing is (a Takeout, a folder, an old library), with a Look at it button for each. The job tiles sit below it, and the separate first page of Start here is gone.
  • A slimmer header. The folder path and the free space share one line, and the green Ready strip only appears when it has something to say. The Help me button is now Something wrong?, so it cannot be mistaken for Help me choose.
  • Quick jobs are a row of pills on Fix, and the dashed box inside the options is gone, so the options sit in one clean frame.
  • Past runs has a tile under Tools, and the runs use the same names as the tabs: Fix my photos, Move my photos, Tidy up, Look-alike photos and so on.
  • Small fixes. Notes on result cards no longer look squashed, the five-styles table names every option correctly, the Count my photos window has a Close button, and source rows no longer break words in the middle.
  • On the website, every screenshot is retaken for these screens.

  • Switch to the finished library. When a Fix or Move copy finishes, the result card says where the finished library is and offers to make it your source, with a fresh folder for the next result. Your original folders then become your backup.

1.0.45

18 October 2026

Every option now says what it did and opens the exact files, results finish with a clear All done, and Move can leave non-photos behind.

  • Every option explains itself. After a preview, each option on Fix, Tidy, Videos, Move, Apple Photos and old libraries shows what it did or would do, with a count. Press Show the 108 to open the exact files, dates before and after, albums, batches or folders behind that number, even for options you left off.
  • A clear finish. A real run now ends with a green All done bar, and the result card has one button row, a before-and-after strip and a Details window with tabs.
  • Move got smarter. Arrange by date and event (2025/2025-04, events such as 2025-04 Japan), old libraries handled in the same place, Photos and videos only with an optional Other folder for everything else, and grouped options that match Fix and Tidy.
  • Fix applies every option you tick. Time zones, folder dates, place names, GPS tracks, motion photos and the rest were being dropped before a run. They now reach it. There is also an optional Look for damaged files first step.
  • Help me choose now sits beside Preview, and the window uses the room on a big screen instead of a narrow column.
  • Check my photos shows its findings again and no longer clutters Past runs with a preview folder for every step. Clear old previews moves old preview folders to the Trash.
  • Fewer glitches. The page no longer fades in and out when you change tab, option lines go stale when a setting changes, and the iCloud service check and the Look at my files result are repaired.

Preview and Do it for real stay in view, quieter setup, one title on each tool, and a much fuller All tools page on the website.

  • On the website: a new, fuller All tools page covers every tool in the seven tabs, with what it does, whether it can change your files, how to use it, and a picture of each.

Download 1.0.35

1.0.34

16 October 2026

One Preview button, one result card with every file listed, arranging photos into folders by date and event, and old libraries now live in Move.

  • Preview, then Do it for real. The “Preview only” tick box is gone. Every tool has one button: Preview. When you like what you see it becomes Do it for real, and it goes back to Preview if you change a setting, so you never run for real on settings you have not seen.
  • One result card, with every file. Move, Fix, Easy fix, Tidy, Videos, Apple Photos, merging similar folders, setting photos aside and the old library copy all end in the same card: a headline, a few facts, where it went (a link), an undo line, and a list of every file with where it came from, where it went and what happened, in plain words. The list shows one row each, 20 at a time, loads more as you scroll, shows the full path when you hover, and opens Finder when you click.
  • Arrange photos into folders by date and event. In Move, tick Arrange them by date and event too. Pictures are filed by the date they were taken (2025/2025-04 by default; year only, year, month and day, or one folder per month are one click away). A folder with a real name, such as Japan 2025, stays together as an event with its date first and Japan 2025 and 2025 Japan become one folder. A picture with no date goes in _undated if you prefer. Preview first, and Undo is in Past runs.
  • Old libraries are in Move. Add an old iPhoto, Aperture or Photos library under What to work on and Move says so, reads its database, and offers the same choices: arrange by album, year, month or day, duplicates, copy gently. The Rescue tab opens Move.
  • A new Home and seven tabs. Home has Start here and six jobs. The tabs are Home, Fix, Tidy, Move, Check, Rescue and Tools. The boxes at the top are now What to work on and Where to save, with one progress strip and one status bar, and confirmations appear inside the app with how many, how big and whether it can be undone.
  • Past runs has filters and search and fewer buttons. Tools is grouped by what you want to know. Compare is one card. The Add picker opens inside the app, so its sidebar and your drives work.
  • Fixes. A folder named like a Photos library but holding plain files is treated as a folder. Tiles no longer flicker or go dark when you hover them. The User guide is rewritten for the new tabs. This release also includes the changes from 1.0.29 to 1.0.33, which were never published on their own.

Every screen remembers its last result, and Compare two places can tag, set aside, trash or delete the identical files from one side.

  • Every screen remembers its last result. Come back to Compare two places, the Old library extractor, Find damaged files, Fix, Tidy, Similar and the rest, and the last result is there with a “Where you left off” note, even after you quit the app. Compare’s Clear and Copy buttons still work on a restored result.

Download 1.0.28

1.0.26

12 October 2026

Compare two places can now clear the identical files from one side, and the old library copy gets Keep trying and Clear the copied originals.

  • Compare two places (it was called Compare libraries) takes any two folders or libraries. After comparing you can remove the identical files from one side: set them aside in a folder (safest), move them to the Trash, or delete them for good (you type DELETE). Every file is compared byte for byte with its twin just before it is removed, and the other side is never touched. It will not clear an Apple Photos library.
  • Old library copy: Keep trying retries only the files that failed. Clear the copied originals moves the originals that are already safely copied out of the old library, to the Trash or deleted for good, after a preview with the count and size. Only files whose copy is in your folder and about the same size are touched, and a list is saved.
  • A failing drive no longer holds the copy up. If the drive does not answer for 90 seconds, the files it stuck on are skipped and listed, the copy carries on, and they are tried last next time. Copy gently copies at about half speed with a rest after every file, for a struggling drive.
  • 39 more Apple log lines explained in Live watch, most as honest low-confidence readings with their sources. The status bar no longer covers the bottom of a tool, and the app saves a hang report if it stops answering.

Download 1.0.26

1.0.24

11 October 2026

The old library copy skips files that a failing drive sticks on, and carries on.

  • The old library copy no longer gets stuck on a bad file. If the drive does not answer for 90 seconds, the files it stuck on are listed as skipped and the copy carries on with the rest. Running the copy again tries them last, for a short time each, so they cannot hold up the others. If 12 reads end up stuck in all, it stops cleanly and says so.
  • You are told what happened. The summary says how many files the drive stuck on, and they appear in the failed list (files_that_failed.csv) with the reason.

Download 1.0.24

1.0.23

11 October 2026

A gentler copy for struggling drives, 32 more Apple log lines explained, and the status bar stops covering the window.

  • Copy gently. A new option in the Old library extractor copies at about half speed and rests after every file, for a USB drive, cable or hub that is struggling. Running the copy again skips what is already copied, so it is safe to turn on part-way through.
  • 32 more log lines explained. Live watch now recognises the iCloud Drive thumbnail errors, the photo-analysis text-encoder lines, the iCloud transfer (MMCS) errors, the Photos library-state and extended-attribute lines, and more. Most are honest best readings at low confidence, with what was searched and what could not be found written beside each. Two existing entries now match whatever error number your Mac shows.
  • The status bar no longer covers a tool. A tool such as the Old library extractor now ends above the bar while a job runs, instead of running underneath it.
  • If the app stops answering, it now saves a report of what it was doing (in Documents / Photo Clinic / Hang reports), so the cause can be found.
  • The status bar title goes back to the task. Click the name of the running job to return to it. The Last used tools take the same colours as their cards.

Download 1.0.23

1.0.21

11 October 2026

The old library copy keeps going when a drive fails, and the Photos tools are coloured by theme.

  • The old library copy copes with a failing drive. A file that will not copy after a couple of quick tries, or that the drive does not answer for within two minutes, is set aside and the copy carries on. At the end the set-aside files get a second, more patient go, and anything still failing is listed by name with the reason, in the summary and in a saved list (files_that_failed.csv). Before, a bad file could use five retries and very long read timeouts, the counter only updated every ten files so it looked stopped, and after three bad files in a row every later file failed at once while the count still climbed.
  • Run it again to finish. Running the same copy again skips what is already there, even if an earlier run was cut off before it could save its record, and a progress line shows how many files are waiting for a retry and the file in hand.
  • The status bar no longer cuts off the window. The page leaves room for the bar along the bottom while a job runs.
  • Photos tools are coloured like Home: purple for fixing, green for cleaning, orange for moving, blue for monitoring and teal for guides, with a small key.

Download 1.0.19

1.0.18

10 October 2026

iPhoto and Aperture libraries can be extracted, the Add button works, and Count and Compare say more.

  • Old library extractor reads iPhoto and Aperture libraries (the kind with Database/apdb/Library.apdb, including .migratedphotolibrary), not only the Photos 1.x layout. It copies your original pictures into folders named for your albums and iPhoto events, with keywords, ratings, titles, dates and places written in. Edited versions, people and faces are not read for this kind of library yet.
  • Move my photos, Tidy up and Fix my photos now stop at an iPhoto or Aperture library and point you to the extractor, because they would otherwise treat it as a heap of files and bring in its thumbnails, caches and database files.
  • The Add button works. Versions 1.0.12 and 1.0.13 showed the dialog but never returned your choice. One dialog now takes folders, Takeout zips and Photos libraries. A folder that holds a mix of libraries and other things can be split into its parts, and a library in the older layout that is still named .photoslibrary is recognised as an old library.
  • Count tells you what is in your folders. A What is in table by file type and a By folder table, with plain descriptions. A library's own thumbnails and edited copies are no longer counted as photos, and your Apple Photos library shows the size of its originals.
  • Fix my photos copies your other files (PDFs, documents) beside the photos, so the copy is complete, with a tick to turn it off and Undo. A finished preview has a Do it for real button. Compare libraries says when its picture check stopped at 30,000 pictures and can look at every picture. Start here offers to compare two sources you chose.
  • Keep sync running has a shield button beside Live watch. The menu shows the release number. Decorative looping animations are switched off, which should stop the flicker. Folders with a single year in the name go under that year when you tidy.

Download 1.0.13

1.0.11

9 October 2026

A guided Start here, a Status tab, Keep sync running for when you are away, and tidier logs and reports.

  • Start here. Choose what you have (a Google Takeout, an old library, a folder, your Photos library or a drive that struggles) and Photo Clinic works out what it is, lists what it found grouped by the tool that deals with it, and makes a step-by-step plan. Each step says why, checks that the Target, free space and tools are ready, and works on the copy once an earlier step has made one. Progress is remembered, a green banner says when a step finished, and a Back button returns you to the plan.
  • Status tab and a live status bar. A Status tab in the side pane shows one bold number per check (for example Library 95/100), with grey rows you can check with one tap, and an Apple system status row. While anything runs, a bar along the bottom shows progress, counts, time left and Stop.
  • Keep sync running (Tools, Monitor) watches iCloud while you are away. It does not restart on a timer: it restarts the iCloud Photos sync or iCloud Drive only when the sync backlog is stuck or a service is pinned at high CPU, gently, at most once every two hours, never while iCloud is throttling, and it stops and tells you after three restarts that did not help. It can keep the Mac awake while plugged in, and has a Check before I leave list.
  • Apple system status asks Apple's public status page whether anything is not green, only when you press it. Which file is iCloud Drive busy with? lists files changing right now in iCloud Drive, Desktop and Documents.
  • Log cards are tidier. Each is one summary line with a coloured count, the process's CPU, a one-line description and a chevron to open the detail. Live watch has an issues-over-time chart, and the filter and add-unrecognised buttons sit beside Start. New one-tap restarts for the iCloud sync service and the Photos analysis service, and a reversible Reset iCloud's local cache. Fixed: Restart iCloud Drive was restarting the wrong service.
  • Tidy up can put date-named folders under their year (13 Sep 2014 goes in 2014), on by default. Old library extractor is one click from the look to the copy, and shows where it will copy.
  • Reports and spreadsheets now live in Documents/Photo Clinic (Runs, Previews, Spreadsheets, Receipts). Files earlier versions left on the Desktop and in Documents/Shoebox Reports are moved there once, and past runs still open.
  • Sharing is more reliable. Lines waiting to be shared are kept across restarts and the daily limit, the sender starts when the app opens, and the report inbox lists what is waiting, with Don't send and Send now.
  • Versions 1.0.7 to 1.0.10 were not released, so this includes everything since 1.0.6. The Target editor now matches the Source, a Home card is back among the tool cards beside a Last used group, and the window opens close to the height of the screen.

Download 1.0.11

1.0.6

9 October 2026

Get photos out of an old library, find damaged files, and copy only the good ones.

  • Old library extractor (Tools, Utility). Turns an old Apple Photos, iPhoto or Aperture backup into a clean folder of copies, by album, year or year-month, with edited versions beside the originals and keywords, titles, ratings and places written into the copies. It skips exact and probable duplicates, shows a preview first, and has Undo.
  • Find damaged files lists photos and videos that are cut short, empty or will not open, and files that make a drive stop answering. It does not freeze when a drive stalls. Each row has copy-name, show and open-folder icons, and you can tag the damaged files in Finder.
  • Copy the good files copies the healthy photos and videos, with their .json files, to your Target and leaves the bad ones behind, with an optional move once each copy is verified. It is safe to run again after an interruption, and a stall lets you skip, try the next files or retry.
  • Log messages explain more. Ids in a log line get names where your Mac knows them (shared albums, photos) or a plain explanation where it does not, shown beside the untouched raw line with copy icons. Every finding has a line breakdown and a Plain or Technical wording switch.
  • Catalogue: 283 entries. 23 entries were researched again and 10 new ones added. Most are still marked low confidence and unverified, and each entry says what is inference.
  • Calmer and tidier: lines you have already sent are shown as sent, the Target shows on the Tools tab, sources are a tickable list, and the app repaints much less while it works. Live watch no longer hangs when a drive has stopped answering.
