1.0.1 Latest

6 October 2026

The monitor moves into the app, with a calmer look.

  • Photos Health tab. A reading every 15 minutes while the app is open, with trends and a plain verdict: healthy, downloading, merging, waiting on sync, errors or stuck.
  • Tells you when macOS is blocking it. If a permission is missing, it names the program, shows since when, and opens the settings page for you.
  • Check Photos remembers. The iCloud queue check now compares against the Photos Health history, so you no longer have to wait hours between two taps.
  • A calmer header. An arrow between where your photos come from and where they go, and a star icon for favourites.
  • A simpler five-styles table. It opens showing only what differs between the styles.
  • Clearer updates. The update banner now says why an update was refused, for example when a job is still running.

1.0.0

5 October 2026

The first signed and notarised release.

  • Fix a Google Takeout: dates, places, captions, people and favourites restored from Google's files, read straight from zips, with time zones, Live Photos, edited copies, albums and Motion Photos handled.
  • Dates and places from every clue: file names, folders, neighbouring photos and GPX tracks, plus offline place names for about 144,000 towns and cities.
  • Combine and tidy: merge folders, remove exact copies, review look-alikes, convert old videos to MP4 with verification, and a library health score.
  • Check Apple Photos and iCloud: upload progress, log reading, live watch, a sync meter, a disk-space check, about 160 known problems and 17 step-by-step fix guides.
  • Move to Apple Photos in batches, oldest first, waiting for iCloud between batches.
  • Five styles from Safest to I like risk, preview first, copy by default, stop and undo, reports for every run.
