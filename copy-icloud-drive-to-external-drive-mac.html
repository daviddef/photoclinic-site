Copy iCloud Drive

How to copy iCloud Drive to an external drive on a Mac

Finder fails on big iCloud Drive folders: files that are not downloaded do not copy, and you cannot tell what is missing. Photo Clinic copies slowly and carefully, shows you the gaps, and fills them in.

Download for Mac

Why copying iCloud Drive is hard

  • Files that are only in iCloud are not on your Mac, so Finder has to download each one first, and it gives up or skips some.
  • Hundreds of gigabytes fill your Mac before they reach the drive.
  • When the numbers do not match, you cannot tell which files are missing.
  • Any stop, sleep or error means starting again.

What Photo Clinic does differently

  • One file at a time. It asks iCloud to send the file, copies it, checks the size, then frees the space on your Mac again (you can turn that off).
  • Resumable. Running it again skips what is done. Close the app and it offers to continue next time, or can carry on by itself.
  • Honest numbers. Live speed over the last minute and time left, based only on what still has to be copied.
  • Pick folders from a tree with the size and how much is only in iCloud shown on every folder.
  • One clear line per file in Activity, with a link to the copied file in Finder.
  • Checks the space on your drive and your Mac, and waits instead of failing when it is nearly full.
  • An optional green Finder tag on each original once its copy is checked.

Already copied some of it? Fill in the gaps

If you tried a copy before (with Finder, another backup app or Photo Clinic itself), press Compare with what is already copied. It looks at your copy, wherever you point it, and shows:

  • how many files are in iCloud and how many are already in the copy, with sizes
  • which are missing and which are cut short (same name, wrong size)
  • folder by folder, what is copied and what is missing
  • files that are only in your copy, not in iCloud

Then Copy the gaps copies only what is missing or cut short. Files that are already there, at the right size, are left alone.

Step by step

  1. Choose your folders. Open Copy from the cloud on Home and tick the iCloud Drive folders. Open a folder to choose something inside it.
  2. Choose Where to save. Pick a folder on your external drive.
  3. Compare. See what is already copied and what is missing.
  4. Copy. Press Copy the gaps (or Copy the ticked folders the first time). Watch the speed and time left at the top of the screen, and press Stop whenever you like.

When some files fail

A big copy always finds a few problems. Photo Clinic groups them by cause, so 50,000 messages become a handful of lines you can act on:

  • Moved or deleted since the scan. Harmless: nothing to do.
  • iCloud did not send it in time. Check the internet, then try again.
  • Not allowed to read or write. A button opens the macOS privacy setting.
  • The drive is full or was disconnected. Fix it, then continue.
  • The file could not be read. The file or the drive may be damaged.

Each group has Show the list, Copy the list and Try these again, and the full list is saved as a spreadsheet.

Questions

Does it change anything in iCloud?

No. It only reads iCloud Drive. Files it brings down for a copy are taken off your Mac again afterwards (if you leave that option ticked), and the optional Finder tag is only a label.

How long does it take?

It depends on your internet speed. Photo Clinic shows the current speed and an honest time left. The copy can be stopped and carried on at any time, even after a restart.

Where do my files go?

Into a folder called iCloud Drive inside the folder you choose, keeping your folder structure.
