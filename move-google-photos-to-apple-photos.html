How do I move my photos from Google Photos to Apple Photos?

Short answer: export a Takeout, fix the dates and places, then send the photos to Apple Photos in small batches and let iCloud catch up. Keep your Takeout until you have checked everything.

Download for Mac

The steps, in order

  1. Export a Google Takeout. Choose Google Photos, and download every zip file. Do not unzip them yet.
  2. Put the dates, places and captions back. The photos come out without them. Photo Clinic reads Google's .json files and writes everything into copies of your photos. See Google Takeout.
  3. Tidy and remove duplicates. Edited copies, album copies and look-alikes pile up. See Duplicates and tidy-up.
  4. Send a small test first. Import about 20 photos into Apple Photos and look at them. Photos has no undo for imports.
  5. Send the rest in batches. Oldest first, waiting for iCloud to upload between batches. See Apple Photos.
  6. Watch it finish. A big library can take days. Monitor shows whether it is syncing or stuck.

Things that catch people out

  • Photos show the download date, not the date taken, unless you fix them first.
  • Live Photos arrive as two files and need pairing.
  • Importing everything at once can fill your Mac and confuse iCloud.
  • Videos in older formats (AVI, MKV, WMV) cannot be imported until converted.
  • iCloud is slow to catch up on a big library. Days are normal.
Do not delete anything from Google yet. Keep your Takeout and your Google library until you have checked Apple Photos and iCloud.
