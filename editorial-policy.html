How we write

How we write and check our guides

What goes into the guides on this site, how we check them, and how we handle corrections.

Download for Mac

What we write about

Only things we have actually run into while moving, copying and checking large photo libraries. We would rather write less and have it be right than write a lot.

How we check

  • Steps are tried on a real Mac, on a real library, before they are written down.
  • Numbers in examples come from real runs, with names and paths changed or removed.
  • Claims about how other products behave are checked against the product's own documentation where it exists.
  • Where the app and the guide disagree, we fix whichever is wrong.

Saying how sure we are

Apple does not document most of the messages that Photos and iCloud write to their logs. Where we explain one, we say whether it comes from Apple's documentation, from what we observed, or is our best reading. If we are not sure, we say so, and we do not present a guess as fact.

Corrections and updates

Each guide shows when it was last reviewed. When something changes, such as a new version of macOS or a change in how Google exports photos, we update the guide and the date. To report a mistake, contact us.

Advertising

Ads, if shown, never decide what we write. We do not take payment to recommend anything, and Photo Clinic itself is free.
