Not in Photos yet

How to find which photos are not in Apple Photos yet

Before you drag a big folder into Photos, find out what Photos already has. It saves hours, and it stops the “Cannot import” and duplicate messages.

Download for Mac

The problem

  • Photos does not tell you which pictures in a folder it already has.
  • Importing thousands of files you already own wastes hours and disk space.
  • A big drag-in can end with “Cannot import” or hundreds of duplicate questions.
  • Pictures that exist only in iCloud cannot be checked by looking at files on your Mac.

What Photo Clinic shows you

Choose a folder and your Photos library, and press Check what is missing. You get four numbers:

  • Pictures in your folder
  • Already in Photos, the same file
  • Photos has the name, but a different file, which could be an edited, resized or better copy
  • Not in Photos at all

See them side by side

Press Show me to see your file next to the picture Photos holds under that name, with their sizes, a link to your file in Finder, and a button to show the Photos one in the Photos app. A short note says what it probably means, for example that a RAW file is usually stored with the JPEG of the same name.

Then what?

  • Copy the missing ones into a new folder, ready to import. They are filed in a folder named after the album that fits best (the album most of that folder's other pictures are already in), so Photo Clinic's album-from-folder import files them correctly.
  • Move the ones Photos already has aside into a folder called _already_in_Photos inside your own folder. Nothing is deleted, you can undo it, and you delete that folder yourself when you are sure.
  • Send them to Apple Photos in careful batches, with a small test first.

How it knows

Two ways, both read-only. It compares the content of your files with the full-size originals Photos keeps on this Mac. And it reads Photos' own records, which list every picture's original name and exact size, so it also recognises pictures that live only in iCloud. Photos you deleted (in Recently Deleted) are not counted as being in the library.

Honest about what it cannot know. A different file under the same name might be a better copy, a smaller copy, or a different picture that shares a camera number. Photo Clinic shows both sizes and leaves the choice to you.
