All tools

Every tool, in plain words

Photo Clinic is a set of small tools that work together, in seven tabs. You do not need all of them. Start with Start here on the Home tab and it will point you to the right ones.

Home: not sure where to start?

Start here

Choose what you have (a Takeout, a folder, a library, an old library). Photo Clinic works out what it is, what is wrong with it, and makes a plan with the steps in order.

Check my photos

One tap. Checks your folders, your Photos library or both: is it healthy, is it in iCloud, is anything going wrong? More

Fix

Easy fix

Puts the real dates, places and captions back, and tidies the files. Five styles from Safest to I like risk. More

Fix details and quick jobs

Every option, or just one job: the JSON fix, dates only, places only, or fix in place (careful: no copy is made). More

Tidy

Tidy up leftovers

Clears empty and junk files, leftover folders and odd names. More

Videos won't play

Turns old video formats into MP4 that plays everywhere. Each one is checked before the old one is replaced. More

Find look-alike photos

The same picture saved twice or at another size. Shows them side by side and suggests a keeper. More

Move

Combine and arrange folders

Merges folders and, if you like, files everything into neat folders by date, keeping events such as 2025 Japan together. More

Send to Apple Photos

Sends your photos into Apple Photos in careful batches, with a preview and a small test first. More

Check

Photos Health

A reading every 15 minutes: is your library syncing, stuck or erroring? With a trend chart. More

Is everything in iCloud?

Counts what is in Photos and what has reached iCloud, and the time left. More

Rescue

Rescue an old library

Gets photos, albums, keywords and ratings out of an old iPhoto or Aperture library, gently, even from a failing drive. More

Find damaged files

Finds pictures and videos that will not open, and copies the good ones to safety. More

Tools

Compare two places

How alike are two folders or libraries? Copy what only one has, and clear what is identical. More

Read the log

Recent Photos and iCloud errors, grouped and explained, with fixes. More

Live watch

Messages from Photos and iCloud as they happen, with the real file names. More

Look up a photo

Turn an id in a log message into the real file name, date and albums. More

Broken thumbnails

Finds photos whose previews are missing or empty. More

iCloud Drive busy files

Which file is iCloud Drive stuck on right now? More

Keep sync running

Disk space

What is using the space on your Mac. More

Preview first, then Do it for real. Every tool that changes files starts with a Preview button that changes nothing. When you like what you see, the button becomes Do it for real. Tools that can remove files ask first, show you the count, and say whether the files go to a folder, the Trash or are gone for good. Every job ends in one card with a list of every file.
