Google Drive

How to download a Google Drive folder, or your Takeout files from an email, to your Mac

Sign in with your own Google account, choose your own folders, and Photo Clinic copies them to your drive. It can only read your Drive, and it carries on where it stopped.

Download for Mac

Download folders from Google Drive

  • Your own account. Sign in on Google's page; read-only.
  • Your own folders. Browse My Drive and Shared with me, or paste a link.
  • Resumable. Run it again and finished files are skipped; a restart offers to continue.
  • Checks at the end that everything arrived.
  • Files that share a name are all kept.
  • Clear problems. Quota, permission and not-found are explained, with what to do.

Got an email full of Drive links?

After a Google Takeout to Drive, Google emails you a list: “View 1 of 43 in Drive”, “View 2 of 43” and so on. Clicking 43 links by hand is slow and easy to get wrong. In Photo Clinic, open Copy from the cloud and the Links tab, and paste the email.

  • It finds every Google Drive and Takeout link, even when the email was copied with its links hidden behind the words.
  • Puts them in order by their “1 of 43” labels and drops repeats.
  • Makes a checklist: waiting, downloading with speed, done, or failed with the reason.
  • Downloads several parts at once (you choose how many) and skips parts it already has when you run it again.
  • Offers to use the downloaded zips as your Takeout source, ready to fix.

Takeout download links (the ones that open in your browser)

Links on takeout.google.com must be downloaded in your browser, and Google asks you to sign in again. Photo Clinic cannot do that for you, but it keeps track: press Open for a part, let it download, and Photo Clinic moves each finished file from Downloads into your folder and ticks it off. Partly downloaded and broken files are left alone.

Step by step

  1. Connect Google Drive. Press Connect. Google's own page opens: choose your account and allow read-only access. Photo Clinic never sees your password.
  2. Choose folders from My Drive or Shared with me, or paste a folder link. Open a folder to choose something inside it.
  3. See the size of each folder and whether your drive has room.
  4. Copy. Several files at once if you like, with the speed and time left. Stop whenever you want and carry on later.

Questions

Is it safe to sign in?

You sign in on Google's own page. Photo Clinic only asks for permission to read, so it can never change or delete anything in your Drive, and the sign-in stays on your Mac. You can sign out at any time and withdraw access in your Google account.

Does it work with links to other services?

It works with any Google Drive file or folder link your account can open. Dropbox, WeTransfer and similar links are listed as skipped.

What if Google stops the downloads?

Google sometimes limits downloads for a day. Photo Clinic says so in plain words, keeps what it has, and carries on when you try again.

What about files with the same name?

Google Drive allows two files with the same name in one folder. Photo Clinic keeps them all, naming the extras Name (dup1).
