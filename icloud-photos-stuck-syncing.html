Answers

iCloud Photos seems stuck syncing on my Mac. Is it slow or stuck?

Short answer: a big library can take days or weeks to sync, so slow is normal. Look for signs that it is not moving at all, and check Apple's own status first.

Download for Mac

Check the easy things first

  • Is Apple having a problem? Check Apple's system status page. Photo Clinic can check it for you.
  • Is the Mac plugged in and awake? Low Power Mode pauses background syncing.
  • Is there free space on the Mac, and room in your iCloud plan?
  • Is Photos open, and is iCloud Photos turned on?
  • Give it days, not minutes, before deciding anything is permanently stuck.

Slow or stuck?

From the outside they look the same. What tells them apart is whether the numbers move over hours. Photo Clinic takes a reading of your library every 15 minutes and shows the trend: the size of iCloud's sync queue should shrink over time. It only calls a library stuck when every sign agrees: the merge has run for hours, the queue has not moved, and the sync process is busy.

What not to do

  • Do not repair the library just because it is slow. Repairing is for real damage.
  • Do not keep restarting services. It can make things worse.
  • Do not delete anything to make it go faster.
  • If it really is stuck, keep the evidence and leave the library alone while you check.
