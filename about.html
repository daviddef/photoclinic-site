About Photo Clinic

Photo Clinic is a free Mac app and a set of plain-language guides, made by people who got stuck moving a large family photo library.

Download for Mac

Why it exists

We set out to move a large family photo library out of Google Photos and into Apple Photos. Dates came out wrong, places and captions went missing, duplicates piled up, iCloud stalled without saying why, and copies of iCloud Drive came out with files missing and no way to see which. Existing tools each solved a piece of it. We built Photo Clinic to solve all of it in one place, and wrote down what we learned.

What it is

  • A free Mac app (Apple silicon) under the MIT licence. Signed and checked by Apple.
  • A growing set of guides that explain the problems in plain words, whether or not you use the app.
  • An independent project, not affiliated with Google or Apple.

Who it is for

Anyone with a large personal photo library on a Mac who wants to know what they have, get it out of one service, put it into another, or just check that it is safe and complete. You do not need to be technical: every job starts with a preview and says what it will do in plain words.

How it is made and checked

  • Every change the app makes starts as a preview, and copies are made instead of moving your originals.
  • The app has nearly two hundred automated tests that run on every change, using made-up photo libraries, zip files and folders.
  • It is tried on real libraries as well as test ones, not only on made-up data.
  • Explanations of Apple Photos and iCloud messages say how sure we are. See how we write.

How it is paid for

The app is free. The website may show advertising to help pay for hosting and development. Ads never appear inside the app, and the app contains no tracking.
