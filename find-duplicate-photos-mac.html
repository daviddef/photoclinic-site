How do I find and remove duplicate photos on a Mac?

Short answer: there are two kinds. Exact copies are safe to remove. Look-alikes need you to choose which one to keep.

Download for Mac

Two kinds of duplicate

Exact copies are the same file twice, byte for byte. A program can find them by their content, whatever they are called. Look-alikes are the same picture saved at another size, or re-saved or edited. Those are a judgement call, because one of them is usually better.

How to choose the keeper

  • A favourite (5 stars) beats one that is not.
  • An edited version beats an untouched one.
  • More pixels (higher resolution) wins.
  • A bigger file wins, because it is less compressed.
  • More information inside (date, location, caption, keywords) wins.
  • You can reorder or switch off any rule, and bursts are kept by default.

Comparing two places

If you copied a folder or library and want to clear what is now in both, Compare two places shows what is identical, then lets you clear those files from one side. It compares each file byte for byte with its twin first, and you choose whether they go to a folder, the Trash, or are deleted for good. The other side is never touched.

Be wary of anything that deletes duplicates on its own. Always look at the keeper it chose. Photo Clinic shows look-alikes side by side with a suggested keeper and never deletes them automatically.
