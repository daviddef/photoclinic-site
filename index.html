Get your photos out of the shoebox

Photo Clinic fixes the dates, places and captions that Google Photos strips out of your download, and keeps an eye on Apple Photos and iCloud.

Download for Mac

Free · Apple silicon Macs · signed and checked by Apple

How it works

  1. Point it at your Google Takeout. Zip files work as they are, so there is no need to unzip 50 GB first.
  2. Look at the preview. Nothing is changed until you say so, and your originals are copied, not touched.
  3. Press go. Every photo gets its real date, place and caption back, ready for Apple Photos.

What else it does

Combine librariesMerge folders, remove exact copies and find look-alike photos, with a side-by-side review.
Check Apple PhotosSee whether everything has reached iCloud, what is stuck and why, in plain words.
Photos HealthA reading every 15 minutes shows if your library is syncing, stalled or erroring.
Tidy upConvert old videos to MP4, clear junk and empty folders, all verified before anything is replaced.

Private by design

Photo Clinic runs on your Mac. There is no account, nothing is uploaded and nothing phones home. It never changes your Apple Photos library; it only reads it.

Photo Clinic is free and open source (MIT licence). It is an independent project and is not affiliated with Google or Apple.
