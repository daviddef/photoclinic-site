How it works

  1. Tell it what you have. Drop in your Google Takeout zips, a folder, an old library, or point it at your Photos library.
  2. Read the preview. It shows what would change. Nothing is touched yet.
  3. Press go. Your photos are copied, fixed and tidied into a new folder. Your originals stay as they were.
Our promise. Preview first. Copy, do not move. Undo where we can. Nothing is uploaded. You can read how in Safety.
