Fix Google Takeout photos: put back the real dates, places and captions

When you export from Google Photos, the dates, places and captions come out in separate .json files and the photos themselves arrive bare, often showing the day you downloaded them. Photo Clinic reads those files and writes everything back into the pictures, across as many zip files as Google gave you.

  • Dates in the right local time, places, captions, people and favourites.
  • Live Photos paired again, hidden videos taken out of Motion Photos, edited copies matched to their originals.
  • Photos with no date get one worked out from the file name, the folder or the photos next to them, and each guess is labelled.

Copy iCloud Drive to an external drive, even when the files are only in iCloud

Big iCloud Drive folders do not copy well in Finder: files that are not downloaded fail, and you cannot tell what is missing. Photo Clinic copies slowly and carefully, one file at a time. It asks iCloud to send each file, copies it, checks it, then frees the space on your Mac again.

  • Carries on where it stopped after a restart, a sleep or a closed lid.
  • Compares with an earlier copy and shows exactly which files are missing or cut short, folder by folder, then copies only those gaps.
  • Shows the speed, the time left, and every file as it goes, with a link to the copied file.
  • Groups any problems by cause, in plain words, with what to do about each.
How to copy iCloud Drive to an external drive →

Download a Google Drive folder, or your Takeout files from an email, to your Mac

Sign in with your own Google account, choose your own folders, and Photo Clinic copies them to the drive you choose. It can only read your Drive, never change it, and it carries on after any stop. Got an email with “View 1 of 43 in Drive” links? Paste the email and Photo Clinic finds every link, puts them in order and downloads them with a checklist.

  • Several files at once, with the speed and time left for each part.
  • Files that share a name in Drive are all kept.
  • Takeout links that must be opened in the browser are tracked for you: each finished file is moved into your folder and ticked off.

See which photos are not in Apple Photos yet, before you import

Dragging a huge folder into Photos wastes hours when most of it is already there. Photo Clinic compares your folder with your Photos library, including pictures that live only in iCloud, and tells you what is already in, what is a different version, and what is really missing.

  • Side-by-side pictures of your file and the one in Photos, with links to both.
  • Copies only the missing ones into a new folder, named after the album that suits them best.
  • Can move the ones Photos already has out of the way. Nothing is deleted.

Find and clear duplicate photos, safely

Exact copies are found by their content, not their name, and moved into a spare folder, never deleted. Look-alikes (the same picture at another size or lightly edited) are shown side by side with a suggested keeper, and the same picture saved in more than one format is found too.

  • You can see both pictures, tick the one you prefer, and check again afterwards to be sure it worked.
  • Everything can be undone from Past runs.

Find damaged or unreadable photos, and rescue the good ones

Photos that stopped short, empty files, and zips that did not finish downloading are found before they cause trouble, with the reason for each. If a drive is failing, Photo Clinic copies what still reads, gently, skips the stuck files and tries them last.

Organise a messy pile of folders, zips and old libraries into one dated library

Point Photo Clinic at a drive full of Takeout zips, loose folders and old libraries. It works out what each part is, copies everything into one library filed by year and month, keeps one copy of each picture and leaves your originals alone.

Check everything in one scan and get a plan

One button reads your folders and your library and gives a health score out of 100, then a plan: what would be fixed, what is duplicated, what is damaged, which videos will not play. Each line has Show me and Do it now, and you can check again to be sure it is done.

Send photos to Apple Photos in careful batches

Photos has no undo for imports and can choke on a big one. Photo Clinic sends your library oldest first in batches, keeps Live Photos together, turns folders into albums, and waits for iCloud to keep up before the next batch.

Make old videos play: convert AVI, MPG, WMV, MTS and 3GP to MP4

Old camera and phone formats do not play everywhere or import into Photos. Photo Clinic converts them to MP4, without re-encoding when it can, and keeps your original or moves it aside, as you choose.
